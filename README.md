# Peter Rozic – Engineering Portfolio

This repository showcases a collection of engineering projects developed throughout my academic and professional experience. It includes work in electric vehicle design, diagnostics, CAD prototyping, robotics, and industrial electromechanical systems.

## Projects Included

### 1. Electric Go-Kart Construction
Complete design and build of a competition-sized electric go-kart, including chassis modeling, structural analysis, motor integration, battery testing, and GUI development.

### 2. Remote-Controlled Telepresence Robot
University group project focused on designing a mobile robot controllable over the internet using SSH, enabling remote presence during restricted-access situations.

### 3. SolidWorks Prototyping – Nerf Mechanism
3D modeling and prototyping of a Nerf-style mechanical system using SolidWorks. Includes full assembly modeling and component design.

### 4. Automotive Diagnostics – Antis SRL
Technical warranty diagnostics for multiple automotive brands. Includes photographic evidence, failure analysis, and technical reporting.

### 5. Electric Quad & Tricycle Optimization – Arcomex SRL
Consulting work involving inventory creation, battery selection analysis, autonomy evaluation, and feasibility studies for electric vehicles.

### 6. Laser Printing System Diagnostics – Xerobol SA
Hands-on diagnostics and maintenance of Xerox industrial printing systems, including ROS (Raster Output Scanner) analysis and xerography fundamentals.
