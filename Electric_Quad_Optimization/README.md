# Electric Quad & Tricycle Optimization – Arcomex SRL

## Overview
Consulting work focused on improving the autonomy and reliability of electric quadratracks and cargo tricycles. Includes inventory creation, battery selection analysis, and feasibility studies.

## Work Completed
- Full inventory of electric vehicle spare parts
- Analysis of autonomy limitations in La Paz (high altitude)
- Comparison between lead-acid and lithium battery systems
- Selection matrix for optimal battery configuration
- Evaluation of thermal limitations in motors and controllers
- Final recommendation based on cost and geographic feasibility

## Key Findings
- Lithium batteries significantly improve voltage stability and autonomy
- Continuous operation requires improved motor cooling
- Battery replacement is not cost-effective for La Paz but viable in Santa Cruz
