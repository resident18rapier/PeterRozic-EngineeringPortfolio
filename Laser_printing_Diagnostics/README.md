# Laser Printing System Diagnostics – Xerobol SA

## Overview
Hands-on diagnostics and maintenance of Xerox industrial printing systems. This project focuses on understanding and servicing the ROS (Raster Output Scanner) and the xerographic printing process.

## Work Completed
- Disassembly and inspection of ROS components
- Analysis of polygon mirror motor, SOS sensors, and laser diodes
- Cleaning and alignment of optical elements (lenses, mirrors)
- Diagnosis of mechanical and electrical failures
- Study of xerography principles and charge-transfer mechanisms

## Skills Demonstrated
- Electromechanical diagnostics
- Optical system maintenance
- Understanding of industrial printing technology
- Technical documentation
